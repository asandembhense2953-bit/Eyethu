About our roadhouse

Raymond and Thandeka Mpala opened Eyethu Roadhouse so their community would stop driving an hour just to share a proper meal. It's the first modern, affordable roadhouse of its kind in Uitval — built for birthdays, baby showers, graduations and Sunday tables.

More than 60% of what lands on your plate comes from local growers, bakers and suppliers, and every person on our floor calls Uitval or a neighbouring community home. Eyethu means "ours" — and that's exactly how it feels once you're through the door.
